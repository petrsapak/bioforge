## Done
- Copied from bioforge-online: sim, render, builder, cell data, templates and tests. The open world and progression code is dropped.
- `data/arena.js`: arena layout, towers, cores, minion designs and waves, budget, bot designs and names.

- `moba.js`: the match rules.
  - Structures are pinned creatures, and towers are shielded until the tower before them falls.
  - Towers shoot minions first, then heroes.
  - A wave every 30 s.
  - The fountain heals, refuels and regrows lost cells, and burns enemies.
  - Heroes respawn after 15 game s.
  - Bot heroes push their lane, stay out of tower range, and retreat when hurt.
  - Destroying the enemy core wins.
- `sim.js` changes:
  - New cell flags: `c.pin` (doesn't move) and `c.inv` (takes no damage).
  - `regrowCell` split out of regrow.
  - Bonds between two pinned cells are skipped.
  - Substeps cut from 8 to 4: same swim speeds, weapon tests pass, no blow-ups in a 4-minute bot match.
  - A step now takes about 5–6 ms with 10 heroes, down from 12–25 ms.
- UI: the builder's button reads "Start 5v5 match".
  - Match HUD: clock, next wave, team kills, fps, respawn countdown.
  - Health bars and names over units, and a shield ring on locked structures.
  - Event feed, and a minimap with lanes, structures, minions and heroes.
  - M opens the big map; T lets a bot play you. A victory or defeat screen appears when a core falls.
- Tests: `node tests/all.js`, covering match, sandbox, showcase and weapons.

## Low fps (user: "the amount of fps is very low")
- Cause: about 840 ms of sim per real second at 2.5x speed with ten 45-cell heroes. The renderer also looped over every bond once per creature and drew off-screen cells.
- Fixes:
  - Renderer: one bond pass, with off-screen cells culled.
  - Game steps of 1/40 s, and fluid flow every 1/20 s.
  - Math.hypot replaced with sqrt, and a collision cull per cell.
  - Budget lowered to 50 biomass and 24 cells, with a new compact bot roster: Lancer, Stinger, Spitter, Snarer.
  - Swim speeds unchanged.
- Result: the sim needs about 190 ms per real second, about 4.4x less. All tests pass.
