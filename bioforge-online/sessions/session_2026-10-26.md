# Session 2026-10-26: Bioforge Online

## Discussed / decided
- Bioforge-v3 prototype counts as finished, and it becomes the MMORPG **Bioforge Online** in `D:\claude\games\prototypes\bioforge-online\`. The plan is in `PLAN.md`.
- Audience: friends first, then public.
- PvP is Lineage 2 style:
  - Attacking a player outside a hub flags you; dying while flagged costs 5% XP.
  - Killing a non-flagged player gives a red name, and red players can drop cells when killed.
  - Hubs have no PvP.
- Hubs are where you rebuild your creature and trade.
- Tools: no PowerShell. Bash is allowed only for the bioforge-online folder.

## Done
- **Milestone 1, port:** v3 is split into files (sim / render / ui / data) and the tests pass.
- **Milestone 2, open world:**
  - Tidepool map, 12000 × 12000 px, with rocks, rock walls and currents.
  - Camera follows the player; there is a minimap.
  - Spawn zones with respawn; enemies far from the player sleep.
  - Enemy AI: idle, chase, leash, flee.
- **Places pass** (feedback: "world unremarkable, starters too big"):
  - 11 named regions, each with its own colours and enemies.
  - Region name banner when entering a region.
  - 4 caves with tougher enemies inside.
  - The Maw: dungeon boss lair, sealed for now.
  - Leviathan Ring: world boss arena, empty for now.
  - Haven: the start area, where the hub will be.
  - M opens the full map.
  - New small prey: mote (5 cells, passive) and nipper (6 cells).
- **Milestone 3, progression:**
  - Levels 1–20 with an XP curve (30·L^1.7).
  - Kill XP = 15·m^1.3, scaled by the level gap. Two level-1 kills reach level 2, as the user asked.
  - Biomass is 40 + 8 per level; the cell limit is 16 + 3 per level.
  - Cell types unlock by level (Bud at 10).
  - Enemy levels are shown over enemies, coloured by the gap to your level.
  - Level-up banner.
  - Level and XP are saved to localStorage.
- **Game speed:** 2.5x real time (`GAME_SPEED` in `ui.js`). The HUD shows fps. The user said 1.5x was still too slow.
- **AI fix** (feedback: "can't damage low lvl units, they run and regen"):
  - No free heal on returning home.
  - Enemies flee only once per fight, at 60% speed, then fight back.
  - User: "better".
- All tests pass: `node tests/all.js` (progress, sandbox, showcase, weapons, world).
