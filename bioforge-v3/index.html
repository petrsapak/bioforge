Guide
Soft-body cells. Nerves decide when cells act. Oxygen, energy, waste and the hormones that boost or heal spread from cell to cell through the whole body; Hearts speed that up near them.
Cells: left-click place, right-click remove. Cells not connected to the Nucleus are only planned (faded, dashed): they cost nothing and are left out of the creature until you join them to the body. Top of the grid = front.
Grid: mouse wheel zooms, middle-drag pans, F shows the whole grid.
Templates: Ctrl+click cells to select them, right-click a selected cell to save them as a template. Click a saved part in the library to stamp it: Q/E rotate, click to place, right-click or Esc to stop.
Save creature (Edit) stores the whole creature, Bud parts, aims and key bindings included; click it under My creatures to load it.
Click a placed cell to set it up: drag a part from the library onto a Bud to make it grow that part, then drag the part's dots to move it, Q/E turn it around the Bud, drag the arrow tip to aim; a Command takes the number key (1–9) you press.
Builder: hold Space to fire Command and Eyespot cells; the grid shows signals and fluids live. Mouths are fed here.

Battle: W/S swim forward/back, A/D turn, Space fires unbound Command cells, 1–9 fire bound ones, T autopilot, Shift+1–6 view, Esc builder.
Sandbox: no budget. Pick yourself and an enemy in the top bar; click the water to spawn, right-click to remove, R respawns you.
