Ctrl+click cells to select, right-click a selected cell to save. Click a part to stamp it: Q/E rotate, click to place, right-click or Esc to stop. Drag a part onto a Bud to make the Bud grow it. Edit → Save creature saves the whole creature to My creatures; click one to load it (replaces the design). Saving under the same name overwrites.
