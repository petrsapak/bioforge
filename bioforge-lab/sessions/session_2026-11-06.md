  kit's Sugar has no column of its own in the limits line (no cell is built from it), so the Reserves' "4 sugar"
  came from nowhere visible.
- Asked: keep both stores or merge them. The user: "ok keep it but we need to explain it to the player - maybe another
  tutorial session when these things are introduced?"
- Fix in `data/tutorial.js`: two lessons, chained after the Lab tour. Long haul (needs Crevice won): fluids, the Creature
  panel (`needs`), Fat as a sugar tank, the Gill, the Pore, a run step, the O₂, Sugar and Waste bars (`hud-bars`) in
  the run, and the result. Packed lunch (needs Marathon won): the kit (`budget`), Reserves, sugar against fat, a run
  step, and the result.
- Chosen without asking: a player who won Long haul already skips its lesson (the usual rule) and gets Packed lunch's;
  a player who skipped the tutorial gets neither until Replay tutorial. No voice lines yet: `tools/make-voice.js`
  needs the user's ElevenLabs key, and a missing line plays silently. The Sugar column in the limits line is not
  added.
