# Session 2026-11-05: Bioforge Lab (locked dock pieces, the tutorial)

## Discussed / decided
- Asked what to do next; proposed play-testing the 2026-11-03 changes and finishing milestone 12 with the tutorial.
- The dock (user): "I want to have all the cell types in the bottom panel, but the not relevant will be locked
  (greyed out with small lock) so the panel doesn't look so empty."
- The tutorial (user): "I imagine tutorial style when it will guide user on what to click on maybe with voiceover -
  since I still have credits in eleven labs. The tutorial can be first 2 levels. Showing on what to click and what to
  do, and presentation of all the functionalitites in the game." Then picked, from options:
  - spotlight + wait: the screen dimmed around the target, and a step moves on once the player has done it;
  - levels 1 and 2 taught step by step, then short tips for the rest (built as a tour after level 2);
  - voice lines made by a Node script the user runs with their ElevenLabs key;
  - the How to box removed.
- Chosen without asking:
  - Clicks are never blocked, only dimmed around, so a player can always undo a mistake. During a run nothing is
    dimmed.
  - The tutorial is kept per game (`tutorial` joined `GAME_KEYS`) and starts with New game, not with Continue: games
    from before it, and every existing test, get none until Replay tutorial in the settings.
  - A player who has won levels 1 and 2 already skips their lessons and gets only the tour.
  - New game no longer opens the Levels sheet.
  - Settings got Tutorial voice (on by default) and Replay tutorial.
  - A step already done when its turn comes is passed silently; a missing voice file plays nothing.
  - A locked piece's click shows "<piece> is locked. Opens with level N, <name>." with the refuse sound, and its
    hover hint has the same line on top. The hint itself still shows, as the field guide does for unmet pieces.
  - `tests/determinism.js`'s type-name rule now bans only comparisons with a written name (`=== 'mouth'`). The
    tutorial compares cells with the type its lesson data names, like levels with their unlocks lists. The reason
    is in a comment beside the rule.

## Signal values (user: "keep the graphs but add a number that will represent the current value of that signal,
## round to 1 decimal number if it is analogue")
- `probes.js`: each lane shows its latest signal right of the trace, in the lane's colour (`probeValueText`): 0 or
  1 for a lane whose samples over the last 6 s were all 0 or 1 (a Pacemaker, a Latch), otherwise one decimal. The
  trace is 28 px narrower to make room. This covers the run's Signals panel and the builder's Traces window.
- Chosen without asking: on/off is read from the lane's own samples rather than from a flag on the neuron, so a
  sense or a Siphon that happens to sit at exactly 0 or 1 for 6 s shows a whole number too.
- Test: `tests/probes.js` (on/off, one decimal, no samples, a live lane). Headless Edge, Lighthouse run: eyes 0.2
  and 0.3, Pacemaker 0, Siphons 0.3 and 0.2.
