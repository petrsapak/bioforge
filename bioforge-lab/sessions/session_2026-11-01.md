## Why a symmetric creature curves (user: "If I have completely symetrical creature that should just go straing why does it curve?")
- An exactly mirror-symmetric fish (22 cells, checked on pixel positions) with both sides pulsing together keeps
  its heading at 0.000 rad for 22 s: the physics is mirror-exact.
- Causes found: the autonomy test's fish isn't symmetric (hex columns zig-zag); the Pacemaker's halves are uneven at
  rates where a cycle is a whole number of nerve steps (1.6 Hz: 50.88% on against 49.13% for the Inhibitor's side,
  0.53 rad of turn in 22 s; 1.5 Hz: 50.00% each, 0.10 rad); and nothing holds a heading, so any imbalance adds up.
- Details in `PLAN.md`, Known limits (Muscle swimming).

## Muscle swimming, built (user: "yes continue with proposed work")
- Chosen without asking: the decisions below, all measured in scratch copies of the sim before changing the game.
- `nerve.js`: the Pacemaker is on for exactly the first half of each cycle (`cycles - floor(cycles) < 0.5`, at
  each nerve step's own time), instead of the sine's sign at the game step's time.
- That alone didn't fix 1.6 Hz: a cycle there is 25 game steps, and Muscles read only the last of a game step's 4
  nerve steps, so one half still got 13 game steps and the other 12 (52% against 48%). So each cell now keeps
  `stepSignal`, the share of the game step its signal was on (`core.js`), and `actuators.js` sizes Muscles and
  Bloaters from it. The symmetric fish at 1.6 Hz now turns 0.14 rad in 40 s, against 0.53 rad in 22 s; at every
  rate tried the turn is a fixed 0.1 rad from the first stroke that then stays put.
- Driving the Muscles by hand (no nerve system) with a square wave showed what swims: a fin of Joints behind the
  Muscles, and a wave running down the body. Fish that are all Muscle swim tail first (the old Paddler did); fish
  with a Joint fin swim head first. A one-cell-wide tail doesn't work. Details in `PLAN.md`, Known limits.
- With real wiring, a fish whose left Muscles hang straight off the Pacemaker turns 1–3.5 rad in 20 s: the
  Inhibitor's side is one fibre further away. With a relay cell on the left, both sides are equally far and it
  goes straight (under 0.05 rad).
- `data/neurons.js`: the Delay goes from 0.05 s in steps of 0.05 s (it was 0.25 s in steps of 0.25 s). A 0.1 s
  Delay in the back-row Muscles of the 15-cell fish gives 34–38 px/s, from 23 without.
- Faster Muscles (snap and relax 24 per s instead of 16) took 12-cell finless fish to 25–34 px/s, but the Snap
  level's reference solution then ran out of time (its Resilin muscle relaxing faster), and the templates with a
  fin gained only 1–5 px/s. Left at 16.
- `data/templates.js`:
  - `muscleFish(rate, backDelay)`: one body for the Wiggler (2.2 Hz, 23.7 px/s, 15 cells, cost 28) and the Paddler
    (the same with 0.1 s Delays, 32.6 px/s). The old Paddler (19 cells, 36 px/s muscle end first) and Wiggler
    (5 px/s) are gone. A Mouth, a Pore and two Gills at the head, a Heart between the Muscles with vessels to the
    Gills, and a fin of five Joints.
  - Eel: the old head on `withMuscleTail` (a relay, Pacemaker and Inhibitor row, four Muscle rows, three of them
    with 0.1 s Delays, a 2-3-2 Joint fin): 40 px/s head first, from 12.
  - Jelly: its Bloater ring was stuck swollen: converting the old touch-wiring had laid fibres in a loop round the
    ring, which kept the signal on. Now `pulsedByNucleus` wires the Nucleus's 1 Hz Pacemaker straight to every
    Bloater, and the bell and tentacles are mirror-symmetric. It drifts 1 px/s; a pulsing bell can't swim here.
  - Manta: its wing row had one Muscle more on the right, so it turned. Now symmetric, Muscles in front and
    Bloaters behind (`withPacemakerRate`, 2.4 Hz). Flapping both wings together can't swim, so its Cilia carry it:
    11.4 px/s, straight (it did 5.5 px/s and turned 0.7 rad).
- `data/tips.js`: the Muscle, Joint and Delay hints say how to build a swimming tail.
- Version 0.7.8 (`data/version.js`, `desktop/package.json`, `CHANGELOG.md`).
- Cost: 80 ms of sim per game s against 79 at the last commit, for the Jet, a Paddler and two Eels with all systems
  for 60 game s (best of 3; the designs differ between the two, so this is about the same).
