## Bars in place of the numbers (user: "I would like to \"replace\" plain numbers and add bars")
- The user: "if you add a cell it will visualize the consumption per second even though the numbers are small, that
  doesn't matter that much".
- The hint's block: one line per fluid with a bar in the fluid's colour. What is burnt, and the waste from it, is
  drawn against the most any cell burns (the Bud, 0.1 per s), so a Muscle's bars are 25% and a Cilia's 12.5%. A
  Gill's O₂, a Pore's waste and a Mender's repair fluid are full bars. The number stays small and dim at the end
  of the line (`cellFlows` gives each flow a `share`).
- The Creature card: three bars (O₂, sugar, waste) for what the body burns per second with every cell working, on
  one scale, which is the largest of the burn, what the Gills make and 0.25 per s (`BALANCE_BAR_SCALE`). So one
  Muscle adds a tenth of a bar. A white mark on the O₂ bar is what the Gills make and one on the waste bar what the
  Pores dump; a mark beyond the scale stands at the end. A bar past its mark, or with no Gill or Pore at all, has
  orange words beside it. The numbers moved into each row's tooltip.
- Chosen without asking: the sugar bar has no mark, since nothing makes sugar at a steady rate; "Sugar lasts N s"
  above it stays.

## Jets that follow the body (user: "it would be great if they would always fire backwards but even if I change the direction")
- The user's idea: "if I move a muscle and the position of the siphon changes then the force would push into that

  the gains, risks and open decisions. It was published as an artifact; nothing in the game changed for it.
- The user: "do it, for both cells, and keep the old rule behind a switch for one version. Also It would be nice
  if the creature had some kind of \"těžiště\" so that the player can see it in the Lab". Built as version 0.8.5.
- `physics.js`: `localForward(cell)` gives the way a Cilia or Siphon pushes: the best-fit turn of the cells bonded
  to it, from the design to now (the same sums that give the creature its heading, over its neighbours alone; one
  square root, no trigonometry). `applySteering` uses it for both cells. It gives null, and the creature's own
  forward counts, in a launched part, for a cell with no neighbour, and with `RULES.thrustFollowsBody` off
  (`data/rules.js`, new, on).
- `cellart.js`: `facingAngle` draws the Siphon's nozzle and puff along its own push.
- `design.js`: `massCentre(cells)`. `builderview.js`: `drawMassCentreMark`, a ring in four quarters 4 grid px
  in radius, on the grid for the cells joined to the Nucleus and on the creature in the Preview.
- Words: the Siphon's and the Cilia's descriptions (`data/cells.js`) and field guide notes (`data/guide.js`), a
  "Mass centre" glossary term.
- Chosen without asking: the helper turn toward the held Muscles (`MUSCLE_TURN_TORQUE`) stays; the Cilia's hairs
  are still drawn on its outer side, only the Siphon's symbol turns; the mark is always on, with no key to hide it.

## What the new rule moved (0.8.5)
- Measured before trusting it: in open water the Jet's, the Seeker's and the Light eel's Siphons point within 0.1°
  of the heading under both rules, and their 10 s runs end at the same px. So stiff bodies are unchanged, and the
  runs that differ are ones a small push tips over.
- The folding tail, the risk named on the page: it doesn't happen. See "Thrust that follows the body" in PLAN.md's
  known limits for the numbers (Tissue, Joint and Shell tails, a Cilia worm).
- The golden tests: 7 of 10 fingerprints changed (squirter, lancer, stinger, spitter, snarer, eel, crab), all
  bodies with Siphons or Cilia. Updated with `node tests/golden.js --update`, since the change is meant.
- Lighthouse with the Jet: it reaches the top wall after 29 s under both rules. Under the old one it stayed nose
  on the wall; now it turns off it and slides along, toward the ring's side. `tests/ghost.js` used this run as
  "swims past the ring"; it now uses First stroke's body, which passes the ring at step 358 under both rules.
- Long haul with a bare Seeker (the level test's wrong tool): it runs out of fuel after 160 s with 127 s near the
  light (old) or 140 s (new) of the 150 s asked. The light comes round again at about 270 s and gave the new run
  its last 10 s, a win. The limit is now 240 s (was 300 s); the reference still wins in 192.5 s with 3 stars.
- Spit with the reference body: 37.6 s (30.7 s before), since every spit kicks the body and the runs part early.
  The par is 40 s (was 34 s) so the reference keeps 3 stars. Chosen without asking, both limits.
