## Lie low won without Calm (user: "lie low level can be won with this creature", a picture of the bare Seeker)
- The bare crossed Seeker (two Light eyes, the Nucleus, two Siphons) won Lie low with 3 stars in 144.5 s (par
  160 s). The same body wired same-side, or not wired, lost.
- Cause: the level wasn't tight on sugar. The bare Seeker burned about 3.4 of its 5 sugar. The always-on Calm gland
  cost about what it saved: it burned 0.005 sugar per s, while Calm (about 0.76 in the body, since Calm fades and
  leaks into the water) cut the Siphons' burn to 0.62 of normal. The reference build burned more than the bare
  Seeker (3.85 of 5). Its two Fat cells didn't help either: the kit's 2 fat went into building them, and their big
  sugar room took most of the starting sugar away from the Siphons.
- A smaller kit alone didn't fix it. At 3 sugar every build without Calm lost, but only one Calm build won (a gland
  and one Fat cell, 149 s); the gland at the back reached only 97 s near the light, and the bare Seeker 90 s.
- Tried in scratch copies: Calm cutting up to 80% of burn, or a cheaper gland making the same Calm. Both made Calm
  builds win at 3 sugar. Chosen without asking: the cheaper gland, since it makes the hint's "costs little" true and
  leaves the meaning of Calm alone.
- Fix: the Calm gland burns 0.005 per s instead of 0.02, with a new cell field `hormoneYield: 4` (default 1;
  `fluid.js` multiplies the hormone a gland makes by it), so it makes the same Calm. Lie low's kit has 3 sugar
  instead of 5.
- Result at 3 sugar: the gland at the back wins in 138 s, at the front in 157 s, at the back with one Fat cell in
  149 s. The bare Seeker gets 90 of 120 s near the light, Tissue at the back 85 s, one Fat cell 40 s, and all lose.
  Lullaby, Moods and Feast and famine still win with 3 stars; the golden tests didn't change. The sim's cost is
  unchanged (one more multiplication per hormone gland per step).
