## Runs differed by frame rate (review finding)
- Cause: `runTimeControlled` cut each frame's time into steps of its own length, while headless runs used 1/40 s.
- Measured before: First stroke with the Jet 17.35 s at 40 Hz, 17.33 s at 60 Hz, 17.31 s at 144 Hz and at 4×;

- After: at 1×, 2×, 4× and ¼× in the fake browser, and at 60 fps and 4× in headless Edge, a run ends on exactly

## Later the same day: marks on the timeline and the ghost (0.8.2)

### Discussed / decided
- The 0.8.1 work above was committed and tagged `lab-v0.8.1` (the user: "commit").
- The user asked how to make the mechanics draw players in, "to want to know more and learn and experiment". Of
  the ideas offered (a ghost and event marks; dissecting creatures; a notebook with predict cards and others) the
  user chose: "I want just #1 that seems like a fun thing to add".
- Chosen without asking:
  - The ghost is the last run of *another* design in the same level, if it lasted 2 game s. A Retry of the same
    design keeps the ghost it had. It is kept in memory only.
  - The ghost is drawn from recorded cell positions (rings, a dashed way, a label), not by running a second world,
    so it costs no sim time.
  - Events are told by comparing the run's state from step to step in a pure file of its own; the sim has no
    hooks for them. Events of one kind within 2 game s are one mark; a run keeps 80.
  - A click on a mark stops one step before its moment, so one step (.) shows it happen.
  - Keys: G for the ghost, Shift with the arrows from mark to mark. A mark's words show in the goal banner for
    1.5 game s; nothing is written into the arena.
  - Version 0.8.2, since 0.8.1 is tagged.
