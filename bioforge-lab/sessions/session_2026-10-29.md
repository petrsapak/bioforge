## Why a level setting (measured)
- At the usual numbers no body layout makes vessels necessary. In a body of about 10 cells every Siphon can touch a store and the Pore, so seeping is always one step. At sustained full work, a Fat behind the Siphons kept them fed for 115 s, and the same Fat plus a Heart and vessels for 130 s, which is close.
- Seeping slowed everywhere to 0.05 or 0.03 per unit of fill difference (from 0.1) would break Long haul's reference build (148 s and 138 s near the light of 150 s) and change every template's fuel use.
- At first the cold slowed only seeping. Then vessels straight from the stores to the Siphons won with no Heart, because an idle vessel (0.4) is 4x seeping at any temperature. Slowing the unpumped vessel flow too makes the Heart the answer.
- Factor tried against 17 builds on Exhaust's chase. At 0.3 a Gill or a second Fat still won. At 0.2 a Gill came within 1 s of winning and vessels alone still won. At 0.1 vessels alone still won (223 s). At 0.075 vessels alone reached 108 s of 120 s. At 0.05, 120 s near the light is reached only by a Heart with vessels to the stores, the Siphons and the Pore (174 s, never starving or poisoned). The best build without a Heart reaches 80 s, and without vessels 17 s.

### The loop (user: "the loop is hearable")
- Cause, measured by decoding the mp3 in headless Edge: the track fades in over its first 5 s (-54 dB rising to -21 dB) and out over its last 4.5 s (down to -85 dB); between, it sits near -20 dB. A plain `loop` drops to silence at the seam.
