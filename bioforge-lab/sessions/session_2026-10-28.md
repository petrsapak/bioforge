## Speed (determinism check)
- In the test harness (Node `vm`), the golden scenario took about 250 ms per 10 game s with our trig and 140 ms with `Math`'s. The cause is the harness: in a `vm` context, every call of a global function goes through a slow lookup, and the angle springs call `arcTangent2` many times per substep.
- Run the way a browser runs it (the scripts in Node's main context): **about 29 ms per 10 game s with our trig and 28 ms with `Math`'s**, so the cost is about 5%. The earlier "69 ms" was measured in the harness too, so the two numbers can't be compared.
- `node tests/all.js` takes about 18 s.
